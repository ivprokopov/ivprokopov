# Hi, I'm Ivaylo  👋

I am a lecturer and researcher at **Sofia University "St. Kliment Ohridski"**. My work focuses on sports pedagogy and competence-based educational models. 

Outside of academia, I am a dedicated applied engineer and an advocate for complete technological sovereignty. I design **open-source hardware**, build local **self-hosted infrastructure**, and actively integrate **local LLMs** into daily technological and educational processes. I believe in solutions that are open-source, privacy-first, and built on solid engineering principles.

### 🔬 Current Focus & Research
- 🧠 **Local AI & LLMs:** Experimenting with and deploying local language models (Ollama, llama.cpp) to optimize workflows and process data entirely on-premise.
- 🔌 **Open-Source Hardware & IoT:** Designing custom printed circuit boards (PCBs) using **KiCad** and SKiDL. Developing home automation systems, IP-KVM modules, and security control panels based on **ESP32-S3** and **ESP32-P4** (RISC-V) architectures.
- 🖥️ **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** Architecting and maintaining an independent server environment, secure networking, and smart home systems.
- 🎓 **Academic Projects:** Knowledge transfer in education, developing innovative methodologies for physical activity (including cricket in schools), and interdisciplinary projects (e.g., Food Forests and permaculture design in sports facilities).
